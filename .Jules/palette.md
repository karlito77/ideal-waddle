## 2025-01-08 - Smooth scrolling with motion sensitivity
**Learning:** Adding smooth scrolling greatly improves the UX of anchor-linked single-page layouts like in this demo, but it can trigger vestibular disorders. Wrapping it in `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)` balances visual polish with necessary accessibility requirements.
**Action:** Always wrap any animation or smooth-scroll behavior in a prefers-reduced-motion check to maintain accessibility standards while delivering enhanced UX.
